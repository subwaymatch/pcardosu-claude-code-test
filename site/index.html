08 · Method and data notes

How these numbers were produced

  • Net spend is the sum of all transaction amounts, including credits and refunds. Gross purchases are shown separately.
  • Period is based on the transaction date. Monthly totals group transactions by the calendar month of the transaction date.
  • Potential duplicates are rows that share the same transaction date, vendor, amount and cardholder. They are flagged for review, not removed, because repeat purchases of the same amount are common.
  • Names and vendors are shown as they appear in the source file. Vendor names were lowercased and whitespace was collapsed for grouping.
  • Reproduce: run python scripts/build_summary.py path/to/PCard-OSU-2014.csv to regenerate site/data/summary.json.
